# Todaywall

A see-through Kanban board for **Specs**. Look at a real wall, pinch to pin, and keep today’s work in three columns: **To do**, **Doing**, and **Done**.

Lenslist **CLAD Summer Hackathon**, weekly theme: **Organize**. Built with Cursor + CLAD.

## Project description

Briefly: what I built, how it responds to the weekly theme, and who it is for.

**What I built.** Todaywall is a standalone AR whiteboard on Specs. Look at a wall: a ghost board follows it until you pinch to pin. Three see-through columns hold task cards: To do, Doing, Done. Pinch-drag a card to move it between columns or reorder it. Add a task with the Specs keyboard or the microphone. Color chips sit on the left; pinch a color onto a card. Drag a card onto the trash icon to delete it. Long titles wrap and the card grows so the text stays inside. Cards save on this device. No Trello, no cloud, no account.

**How it responds to the weekly theme (Organize).** Organize is treated as a place, not an app list. The wall is where today’s work lives. You see what is left, what is in progress, and what is finished at a glance. Moving a card with your hands is how you sort the day.

**Who it is for.** People who think better when tasks sit in physical space: students, makers, and anyone who wants a whiteboard without hanging one on the wall. Made for Specs. It can also be tried in Lens Studio Preview without glasses (mouse click is pinch).
